About the Firm

A chartered IP practice built for East African enterprise

Abdulmalik & Surendra is a chartered patent and trademark practice based on Hindu Street in the heart of Dar es Salaam. We advise inventors, start-ups, family businesses and international brands on how to protect and commercialise intellectual property in Tanzania and the wider region.

Led by partner Surendra Mawji, the firm combines careful technical drafting with practical commercial judgement, so your rights are not only registered but usable: licensed, enforced and renewed on time.

  • Listed with ARIPO, CITMA and AREPTA
  • Local filings and regional or international routes under one roof
  • Plain-language advice, clear scope and no surprises

    FAQ

    Questions we hear often

    Cost depends on the number of classes and the route you choose. Registration typically takes several months from filing, depending on examination and any objections or oppositions. We give a written fee estimate and expected timeline after a short consultation and search.

    A patent protects a new invention or technical solution. A trademark protects the names, logos and signs that identify your goods or services. Many businesses need both, and each follows its own application process.

    It depends on where you trade. A national filing covers Tanzania only, while an ARIPO filing can cover several member states through one application. We compare cost, coverage and risk, then recommend the route that fits your markets.

    Protection periods differ by right. Patents run for a fixed term, usually up to 20 years subject to renewal fees. Trademarks are registered for an initial period and can be renewed repeatedly, so they can last as long as you use and renew them. We track every deadline for you.
